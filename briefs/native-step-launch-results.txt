What changed
- A separate native incoming packet is calculated during sparse_prepare phase14
  before the incoming pad is declared ready. Native P1/P2/wave/noise, waveform,
  tuning, gates, PCM source addresses, merged drum noise and kick share the same
  packet calculations (seq_native_calc.h) as ordinary step lookahead.
- Preparation does not apply the incoming patch or mutate the outgoing notes,
  APU state, fine cache, live lookahead or PCM source records.
- A validated cached-tone installation grants a single-use packet ticket.
  Activation keeps identity/BPM/mute/solo/kit/tuning/gate guards. A stopped pad
  selection or repeated same-pad launch cannot reuse an old install ticket.
- Quantize OFF can finish preparation while the outgoing pattern keeps running,
  before releasing outgoing voices. The demand loop services musical time and
  has a finite budget. It does not reset the timer to hide the preparation cost.
- Fixed-ROM hardware writer commits complete native attacks together.
- IMPORTANT PCM RACE FIX: source records previously became visible to the IRQ
  while the hardware image was still being copied. PCM could therefore start
  one or two blocks early. The final bounded critical window now installs at
  most two source records AND arms the complete packet together. Other native
  steps while the PCM engine runs use the same block pipeline as sample steps.
- Native fine snapshots follow fine_apply clamping for subsequent notes.
- Roll repaint can discard its unuploaded work when a new START press arrives.
  Normal main-loop input handling still consumes the queued press. The next
  roll render rebuilds its discarded cache. No recursive input processing.
- Pure preview helpers accept incoming tuning, sample kit and tempo explicitly.
  Shared bank-zero overlays: A000 live packet, A200 incoming packet, A400 HW,
  A600 incoming build/identity/ticket state. Native-only use of the existing
  protected experimental-delay allocation. No saved-song format change.
- PCM producer/upload order, serial clock service, and pulse-only IRQ disable
  remain intact. Unsupported modes/batches retain the original dispatcher.

Remaining scope ? deferred pending hardware feedback
- Actual DMG audio has not been measured. These numbers are trigger stores and
  first source-buffer upload timing, not waveform-detected audible attacks.
- Inter-channel coherence does not prove alignment with outgoing master clock.
  Foreground work can still make a complete step late. The hardware clock keeps
  running and sequencer catch-up remains; a late launch does not imply permanent
  phase error. Incoming preparation may defer a launch to a later boundary.
- Uncached pad request latency can remain hundreds of milliseconds under load
  (see candidate-handoff120/300.json). This is still material even with coherent
  attacks. Do not market the shorter release gap as zero pad-launch latency.
- Full timer-deadline delivery of a prepared whole packet (with ownership/gate
  cancellation and deterministic late-launch policy) remains future work.
  Wire clock stress passes, but audible alignment to that wire is not yet proven.
