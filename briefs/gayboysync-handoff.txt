Known issue from the user's hardware test:
GAYBOY native sync works well until a new pattern is launched locally on the
SLAVE. Characterize and fix that interaction first; do not assume the failure
is the old pulse-note problem or change the master oscillator as a first step.
Normal master/slave clock and transport already work well in the user's test.

Current native mode sends BPM, start/stop, numbered24PPQN clocks and F2 phase
markers on master pattern launch. F2 aligns the slave's own current pattern at
beat one; it does not remotely select the same pad. The slave normally advances
from incoming clocks. Inspect launch/reset handling and pending clock/phase
state when a local slave launch occurs. Relevant files: src/sync.c,
src/sync_clock.c, src/sequencer.c, src/mixer.c, src/sparse_store.c.

Discussed future ideas, not implemented:
- Master Mixer launches local and remote pad banks independently or together.
- Slave replies with its eight pattern names and active/queued status.
- Names/status are cached and refreshed in bounded background messages, with
  clock traffic retaining priority. The current protocol has no name replies.

Audio-to-master-clock deadline work was explicitly deferred until after the
hardware test. Preserve the successful whole-step timing baseline and the PCM
source/publication race fix. Do not re-enable the pulse-only advance IRQ path.
