EDITBUFFER EXPERIMENT - 2026-10-22
Branch: editbuffer; isolated worktree G:/DEVELOPMENT/GAYBOY-editbuffer.
Stable 1.0 and its online/download builds are unchanged.

Implementation
Two prepared playback snapshots occupy cartridge SRAM bank 2, AD00..BF3F.
Live editing remains separate; complete snapshots become audible at a loop boundary.
Basic native notes use timer dispatch with independent elapsed DIV accounting.
Unsupported synthesis modes, linked operation and long patterns retain foreground dispatch.
Build reports 1636 bytes WRAM headroom and a 512-byte stack reserve.
