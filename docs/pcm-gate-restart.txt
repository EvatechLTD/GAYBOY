PCM gated-sample restart fix - 21 October 2026

Reproduced the hardware report on the old ROM: kit 1 (808), 120 BPM, kick at
step 0 lasting four steps, two different sample attacks at step 4. Neither new
sample reached the PCM fetcher. Other kit/tempo combinations reproduced it too.

The plotted source length was already capped correctly (2,864 bytes for that
kick), so the kick's full source length was not the underlying problem.
drum_enable cleared drumtail before foreground attack preparation. An IRQ in
that preparation window could mark the old voices finished again. Publication
then set the new voices and mixrunning, but left that finished flag set. The
next timer could stop the mixer before priming it, leaving drumon slots occupied
without consuming their samples.

drum_start and drum_fire now clear drumtail in the critical section that publishes
new sample ownership and running state. The interrupt-owned rendering path and
the two-slot polyphony limit are unchanged.

python tools/test_pcm_gate_restart.py
python tools/test_pcm_gate_restart.py --cgb --out build/pcm-gate-restart-cgb.json

Both passed all 48 cases: every kit, 120/150/240/300 BPM, one/four-step gates,
correct source-byte limits, and both following sample attacks reaching the actual
PCM fetcher. The archived pre-fix ROM failed ten of those cases, including the
reported 808/four-step case. Before/after reports are under build/pcm-gate-*.json;
the pre-fix ROM and symbols are under build/pcm-gate-baseline/.

MOD phase/routing/arp, shared-engine, transport, generator and generated-chord
checks passed again. Layout checks retained 2,355 bytes of WRAM headroom and the
512-byte stack reservation. The old pattern-wave and PCM-isolation suites still
stop at their removed page-11 setup and are not counted as passing checks.
