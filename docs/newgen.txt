NEWGEN GENERATOR
===============

Branch: NEWGEN, starting at stripped d0ffbc6.
Open Start > Edit > Generate from the sequence you want to change.
The instrument track or highlighted drum voice is captured at entry.

Up/down chooses a field. Hold A or B and use arrows to edit.
All arrows change one value at a time; left/down decrease, right/up increase.
Each edit previews immediately under the LIVE PREVIEW title. Choose APPLY to
accept or CANCEL to restore. The opening control hint becomes a setting tip
as soon as you navigate or edit. Apply and Cancel align with setting values.
Select also cancels. Opening Start cancels the preview before showing the menu.
Playback continues during previews. The existing track clipboard is used as the
rollback snapshot, replacing its previous contents.

INSTRUMENTS
Role: Melody uses chord tones on quarter-note positions and scale tones between
  them. Bass follows roots, with occasional fifths between quarter notes. Arp
  Line outlines the diatonic triad as separate notes. Chord (P1/P2/Wave) writes
  all three notes simultaneously, for the instrument's existing arp to read.
  Selecting Chord starts with Rhythm CHANGES, Length TO NEXT, Voicing ROOT.
  It leaves the instrument's MONO/ARP mode unchanged. Enable ARP on that
  instrument to cycle its held chord; MONO plays the lowest held note.
Key / Scale: Major, natural minor, Dorian, or minor pentatonic.
  Dorian differs from natural minor only at the sixth degree (raised one
  semitone). Their tonic minor triad is identical, so HOLD I chords match.
  For example, A minor IV is D-F-A; A Dorian IV is D-F#-A. The scale tip
  explains the raised sixth and shared tonic triad.
Chords: Hold I, I-IV, I-V-VI-IV, or II-V-I (the final I lasts two changes).
  Roman numerals identify scale degrees; chord quality follows the selected
  mode. Minor pentatonic uses minor harmony; single-note roles stay within its
  five notes. Set the same key, scale, progression and change rate on accompanying
  tracks to align their harmony.
Change: Quarter note, half note, one bar, two bars, or four bars per chord.
  Bars follow sequencer meter. For a full four-chord progression in a default
  sixteen-step loop, choose 1/4. For one chord per 4/4 bar, set loop length to
  64 steps or more. A too-long change rate is flagged in the menu.
Rhythm: Quarter, eighth, sixteenth, offbeat eighths, tresillo (0,6,12), backbeat
  (4,12), or Euclidean. Positions in parentheses are sixteenth steps in 4/4.
  Instruments also offer CHANGES: an attack at each harmonic change.
Length: 1/16, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 1/1, or To Next. These are maximum lengths.
  Notes stop at the next attack or the loop end; successive attacks do not overlap.
  1/1 is always sixteen steps; bars follow the selected meter.
Contour: Repeat, Rise, Fall, Arch, or Steps. A two-bar motif repeats over the
  progression. The seed chooses its starting point within the contour and
  the small movements in Steps.
Variation: None, Low, Medium, or High. Later phrase endings can change pitch;
  the rhythm remains fixed. None repeats the motif exactly under held harmony.
  For Chord, Contour becomes Voicing (root, first inversion, second inversion).
  Chord variation chooses occasional different inversions, preserving chord
  quality and timing. Its seed is inactive with Variation None.
  Minor pentatonic uses full natural-minor triads for Chord; the menu states
  PENT USES MIN CHORDS. The single-note roles remain pentatonic.
Octave: Base MIDI register. High notes fold down by octaves to stay in range.
Hits: Exact attacks per bar, only used by Euclidean rhythm. Zero means silence.
  Other rhythms show -- because their hit patterns are fixed. Select EUCLID
  in Rhythm first to enable the numeric Hits field.
Seed: Repeatable pitch interpretation; it never randomizes attack positions.

DRUMS
The highlighted name selects the voice even with velocity sliders hidden.
Only that voice's attacks inside the active loop are replaced. Other voices,
other tracks, and notes outside the loop are retained.
Rhythm / Length / Hits: Same timing rules as instruments.
Rotate: Shift the rhythm later by a chosen number of sixteenth steps.
Accent: Flat, Beats, Offbeat, or Bar Start. Other hits play at 75% of the track's
  default velocity. Beats follows the sequencer beat unit.
Variation / Seed: Repeatable softer hits, starting in the first bar, preserving
  the chosen rhythmic mask. With Variation None, Seed shows VAR OFF and the
  menu explains that Seed needs Variation.

STORAGE
Previews count required events before editing the resident 256-event pool.
Each simultaneous chord counts as three events.
A preview that would exceed capacity leaves the previous sequence intact and
cannot be kept. Cancel still restores the original entry snapshot.
