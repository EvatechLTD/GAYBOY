ARP STRESS TEST - 2026-10-22
Branch: cleanup
Fixture: DATA/SAVES/stress-testing/arptest-original.sav (immutable backup).
Latest save contains P2's four simultaneous 16-step notes, the 32-step Wave
lane and original drums. ARPTEST appears in naming scratch; resident pad name
is still P1. Tests use a copy and preserve the original save.

Method
DMG PyBoy, actual saved pattern, playback active on the P2 sequencer page.
120 and 240 BPM; straight and triplet timing; divisions 1/4 through 1/256.
Each case restores the same playback state, settles for 120 frames, records
360 frames of P2 play() calls, then sends/release four navigation keys and
uses the actual A button on the mode header to recover to Mono.
Input acknowledgment is measured through lastpad, not completed screen redraw.
Mode recovery is checked after release and 180 further frames. Six seconds
of idle timing per case is a stress sample, not a long-duration safety proof.

Findings
120 BPM straight: 1/16 navigation acknowledgments about 33..167 ms;
1/32 about 50..385 ms; 1/64 about 1.1..3.1 seconds. At 1/128 and 1/256 the
requested arp rate is missed substantially. Triplets reach saturation sooner.
240 BPM straight: 1/16 navigation about 67..837 ms; 1/32 roughly 1.5..2 sec.
Every tested case eventually recovered to Mono. No permanent lockup reproduced.
The four-note chord's mode recoloring separately costs about 1.5..1.7 seconds
at 120 BPM, even with no full seq_pixels() rebuild; reducing arp speed alone
will not remove that drawing delay.

Provisional playback limits for THIS fixture
120 BPM: fastest recommended straight 1/16, triplet 1/8.
240 BPM: fastest recommended straight 1/8, triplet 1/4.
These keep requested arp attacks at or below 8/sec (125 ms minimum spacing).
They avoid the observed saturation cliff, but do not guarantee fast editing,
low jitter or safety with multiple simultaneous arpeggiators/other heavy work.
A single division ceiling is insufficient because tempo and triplets matter.
Arp range has not been changed; this report records a recommendation.

Implemented BPM limit
Local mixer/sequencer tempo controls cap at 240 BPM. Loaded pattern tempos
clamp to 30..240; incoming serial tempo estimates/messages above 240 are
rejected rather than silently rescaled. Drum timing lookup tables still have
their original entries; extra table entries do not increase hot-path work.

Evidence
build/arp-stress-final.json and .log: complete 28-case matrix.
build/arp-stress.json: first fast-rate matrix before BPM cap.
build/arp-stress-slower.json: slower-rate confirmation.
tools/test_arp_stress.py: reproducible test using --save PATH.
tools/test_tempo_limit.py: mixer, sequencer and loaded-save upper-bound checks.

Validation follow-up: MIDI direct-RX fixture now stops hardware serial after
switching syncmode off by memory. Otherwise an in-flight open-cable transfer
can overwrite injected bytes with 0xff. MIDI launch and edit/MIDI integration
checks pass. No MIDI parser/runtime change was made.
