User requested held drag/resize to stop at screen edges and require Dpad
release/repress to cross, including top/bottom. Replaces the timed edge pause
and the prior B-held viewport pinning with an actual directional release
latch. A fresh press crosses one cell into the neighboring grid. All Dpad
axes must be released to clear the latch. Reversing away remains possible.

Relevant direction stops at its edge: Right at last column, Left at first,
Up at top pitch, Down at bottom pitch. Applies to single/group note moves,
length resizes, and selection box drawing. Drum pitch rows are hard bounds
(all13voices already visible); horizontal drum page crossings work normally.
B resizing from a note body first positions an off-page/edge tail before
length can cross that page, preventing the first resize from skipping an edge.

Explicit crossing permits destination drawing even while A/B remains held.
Synthetic repeats are suppressed until that page is published. Repeat time
and acceleration reset on publication, preventing lag from turning into
queued extra cursor steps. User need not release A/B between gestures.
The destination cursor is first column after Right / last column after Left.
Vertical crossing changes the pitch viewport by one page: Up enters bottom,
Down enters top, constrained by instrument pitch limits.

Ordinary left navigation now goes from global step17 to16 (zero-based16->15)
and lands at the previous page's right edge. Piano focus remains accessible
at pattern step1. Its previous-page shortcut also lands at the rightmost
cell instead of jumping to the previous page's left edge.
