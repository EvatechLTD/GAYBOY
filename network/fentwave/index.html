01 / THE INSTRUMENT

Your SID has
something to say.

FENTWAVE turns the C64 into a place to build sounds, chase patterns and get lost in a patch. The software is real. The dedicated cartridge is the next chapter.

01

A proper little sound lab.

Shape waveforms, pulse width, envelopes, filters and modulation in a dedicated C64 interface.

02

Patterns worth staying up for.

Build music with a sequencer designed around the machine's personality. Less spreadsheet. More happy accident.

03

A cartridge with connections.

Our custom hardware is being developed around MIDI in/out, USB-C, patch storage, an OLED and hands-on buttons.

HARDWARE IN DEVELOPMENT

02 / WHERE WE ARE

Bench first.
Promises later.

The software is running. The custom cartridge PCB has passed its design checks, supplier quotes are underway, and we're working on a printed shell.

Next comes the first assembled board, firmware integration and testing on actual machines. There is no hardware shipping date or final price yet.
